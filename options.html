How your data is used

The extension reads Toggl entries for the current browser-local day and week to calculate daily, weekly, and per-appointment totals, and can automatically choose your most-used active project. It stores your floating button position. It reads the Jira issue summary, description, logged time, and estimates for the timer, side-panel progress, and adjacent copy action. Jira content is copied only after you click the copy button. If Work Log sync is enabled, the stopped duration, start time, issue key, and configured comment are sent to your Jira site, where the remaining estimate is adjusted automatically. Bounded Jira associations and retry records stay in this Chrome profile. Nothing is sent to the extension developer.

Daily use

1

Open an issue or select one from a Jira board or backlog.

2

Use the floating timer button or its adjacent Copy action in your chosen corner.

3

Use Today's appointments in the side panel to replay earlier work and safely stop the current timer.

4

Stop from Jira or the side panel and optionally create, confirm, or retry a Jira Work Log.

Focused permissions

The required side-panel permission hosts these controls beside the current tab. Chrome asks for site access only to the Jira origin entered above. The Toggl token stays in this Chrome profile and is used only by the extension service worker.
